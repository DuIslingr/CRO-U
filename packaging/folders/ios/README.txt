Chaos Rings Omega (CRO-U): your iOS copy goes here - the iPad's ChaosRingsOmegaHDeng.app (1.0.0 or 3.1.0; its data/ holds sys.evt ...),
or the unpacked .ipa (Payload/ChaosRingsOmegaHDeng.app/), or the .ipa itself: the game unpacks its .app
here when it starts (a progress window shows it) and goes on - then the .ipa can be deleted.

The iOS copy is the game; the Vita and Android ones add their voices, music, languages and
art to it (Options, Game). Alone, the Vita copy or the Android release is the game.
It may be here as it was unpacked: one or two folders down is fine. The README's "Your copy".
