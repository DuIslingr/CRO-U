Chaos Rings Omega (CRO-U): a fan translation goes here (optional).

It can be any one of these:
- the translated files themselves, as extracted from the Vita's cro.psarc: sys.evt and the areas'
  .evt files, plus any redrawn .gxt art. Put them in a folder named by the language: ru/ for Russian
  (ru/sys.evt, ru/j001_t01.evt, ...).
- a rePatch folder with the whole translated cro.psarc, in a folder of its own:
  ru/PCSG00499/CRO/resources/cro.psarc.
- the archive either comes in (.7z, .rar, .zip). It is unpacked here into its own folder on the next
  start, and the archive can then go.

It doesn't replace anything. The game's own files stay as they are, and the translation is a
language of its own after the game's eight, e.g. "Русский (fan translation)" in Options > Game >
Language, with English still there.

While it is the language, its text, and any art it redrew, are read from it. A line it doesn't
have shows in English.

It works over the iOS, the Android or the Vita copy.
