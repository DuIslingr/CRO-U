Chaos Rings Omega (CRO-U): your Vita copy goes here - the installed, decrypted PCSG00499 folder (CRO/resources/cro.psarc, CRO/sound/ ...),
or what is in it.
Or a NoNpDrm dump's folder as it was dumped, still encrypted (its app/, and addcont/ beside it for the
Prequel Trilogy's add-on): the game decrypts it into a folder here when it starts (a progress window
shows it) and goes on - it needs the space for the decrypted copy -, then the dump can be deleted. A dump packed in a .zip, .rar, .7z or .vpk: choose it
in the setup the first start shows instead (it unpacks it).

The iOS copy is the game; the Vita and Android ones add their voices, music, languages and
art to it (Options, Game). Alone, the Vita copy or the Android release is the game.
The README's "Your copy".
